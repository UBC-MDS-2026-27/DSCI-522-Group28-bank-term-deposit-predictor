# Code of Conduct
## Our Commitment
We welcome people from all backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience around the world to access this project. Every member who is contributing or viewing this project deserves respect. 

## Expected Behavior
Be respectful and responsible for the GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, and all other project discussions. Create an open space for everyone to ask questions, credit contributions and sources, and show respect to others' time and contributions.
Be authentic and honest, and treat the confidential files carefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior
Harassment (including any types: oral, gestures, languages, and etc.), discrimination, intimidation, sexual attention or language.
Publishing others' work or private information without permission. 

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to the whole project, including pull request, issues, and other project spaces. Further, this also applies to someone who uses the project in other places.

## Reporting and Response
When you are feeling not comfortable, you can tell people that their behavior is making you uncomfortable.

Report concerns privately to the project team. Until a team reporting email is available, contact the DSCI 522 teaching team through a private course message. 
If the issue involves any of the team members, report it to the DSCI 522 teaching team for further resolution. 
Keep sensitive information out of public GitHub issues, pull requests, and comments.

Include what happened, where and when it happened, and any relevant links or screenshots. Share only details needed to review the report. Uninvolved team members or course staff will review it, protect the reporter's privacy as far as possible, and share information only with people who need it to respond. We will share the information only with people who need to respond, and take responsible steps to prevent retaliation.

## Consequences
The response depends on the severity and frequency of the conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent exclusion from project spaces.
